I'm a systems engineer at Draper, where since 2023 I've worked on the interface definitions, models, and requirements that missile defense, guidance, and space programs are built and tested against, and I live in Holliston, Massachusetts, with my wife and our daughter.

## Work

I grew up in Fairport, outside Rochester, New York, made Eagle Scout there, and studied mechanical engineering at the Rochester Institute of Technology, graduating in 2016 and commissioning into the Air Force the same year. My first assignment was at Vandenberg, where I led a small team responsible for more than $70 million in modernization and sustainment of the base's space launch facilities, including an HVAC project that had been the unit's top readiness issue for X-37B recovery, and then moved into launch integration, running the maintenance team that supported Minotaur I and IV booster stacking on both coasts, leading the vehicle stacking for NASA's Orion Ascent Abort-2 test, and lending a hand on Falcon 9 certification.

In 2019 I moved to Hanscom as a program manager for integrated base defense, where I ran acquisition programs and led the proposal evaluation for a $783 million electronic security contract, and I left the Air Force as a Captain in 2021. I then spent two years as a technical project manager at Boston Engineering, leading Navy robotics work that included an automated quality-control system for flight deck coatings and a tether-management vehicle for explosive ordnance disposal, before joining Draper, where my work has run from requirements for DARPA's Otter VLEO satellite and trade studies for Conventional Prompt Strike to MBSE and NLP tooling for Blue Origin's lunar lander, the integration and test architecture for the Trident II guidance system, and interface control on a defense space program. Lately much of my time has gone into tools that check requirements, models, and interface definitions automatically, so that broken traces and mismatched values get caught by software and a reviewer's time goes to the decisions that need a person. Along the way I finished a master's in systems engineering at Johns Hopkins while working full time, with a capstone that modeled an orbital factory for optical fiber.

## Outside work

I run *The Holliston Ledger*, a civic news publication that covers our town government from its public record, and I built ShipWall, a ship-traffic display at my family's cottage on the St. Lawrence Seaway that I keep running remotely. I work wood with hand tools, from my own cabinetry to a banjo, and I bind books, including a facsimile of Robert W. Chambers's *The King in Yellow* that I'm reproducing from scans page by page so that its original typography survives.

I'm also working through a self-directed Great Books program modeled on the St. John's College curriculum, most recently Plato's *Meno* and Aeschylus's *Oresteia*, alongside a lot of science fiction, with *A Canticle for Leibowitz* and the Sun Eater series as the books I return to most. On clear nights I do astrophotography with a small telescope, I go birding and take the bike on trips when there's time, and at home I collect vinyl, share the house with our cats, play the banjo, and have recently gone back to the piano.
