The Automated Orbital Manufacturing System is the concept I developed for my capstone in the Johns Hopkins Master of Science in Systems Engineering program, an unmanned orbital platform that automates in-space manufacturing, beginning with ZBLAN optical fiber, which draws with fewer defects in microgravity than it does on the ground. I used the project to apply the MBSE process to the earliest stage of a system's life, working from lifecycle planning through functional analysis, trade studies, and a verification strategy, so that the early design decisions trace back through the model.

After finishing the degree, I briefed the results to Draper's in-space servicing, assembly, and manufacturing leads, and the requirements and activities from the model now serve as the public demo data for my [requirements traceability pipeline](/work/requirements-nlp/).
